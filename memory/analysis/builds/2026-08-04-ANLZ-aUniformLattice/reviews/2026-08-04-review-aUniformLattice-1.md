### reduce-basis-split (must-fix)

**Claim.** The reduce groups contributions by a key (providerId, metricId, basisVersion) that is FINER than the matrix cell key (providerId, metricId), so two surviving groups compete for one cell slot and the spec never says which wins or how the loser is recorded.

**Where.** S17 + §4 Data model, "The reduce function" (spec lines 153-163), against the matrix key at line 116 and S18 at line 63

**Failure scenario.** google-adwords with two ad accounts: a USD zero-dim KPI card at documentIndex 2 (cost_micros = 100.00) and a EUR zero-dim KPI card at documentIndex 5 (cost_micros = 120.00). Get-CellBasis yields two different basisVersion hashes, so two groups; each has exactly one rank-1 winner. The matrix has one slot for google-adwords:cost_micros. Add an AC modelled on fixture u5c: two zero-dim KPI cards for one metric in two currencies produce exactly one cell, carrying no value and naming both widgets.
